This is not in the first version. It is written down now so it can be built while sync is in progress, without either getting in the other's way.

## The idea

You finish a workout and share it. Your friends see it in a feed, like it and comment on it. If you trained with someone, you tag them and the workout shows on both profiles, like a group activity on Strava. Messages are out of scope.

## Decisions

- **Friends are mutual.** You send a request and they accept. There are no one-way follows.
- **Only finished workouts are shared.** Sharing happens on the finish summary, or later from a workout in history. There is no live view of a workout in progress.
- **A post is a snapshot.** Sharing copies the workout into the post as it was at that moment: title, description, exercises with their brands, settings, notes and sets, records hit, duration and gym. Friends never read your synced tables. Editing the workout afterwards does not change the post unless you share it again, which replaces the snapshot.
- **Posts are public by default.** Friends only and private are opt-ins. Your profile picks the default and each post can change it.
- **Photos and videos are uploaded when you share.** You pick which of the workout's media go on the post, and only those leave the phone.
- **Social stays out of sync.** None of these tables are in SQLite or the sync outbox. The phone reads the feed online, and likes and comments show on screen straight away while the request is sent. Sharing while offline waits until there is a connection.
- **Report and block ship with comments.** App Store guideline 1.2 asks any app with content from other people to let you report it and block whoever posted it.

## Who sees what

| Post visibility | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| `public` | Anyone signed in |
| `friends` | Your accepted friends, and anyone tagged on it |
| `private` | Only you. Tagging is off. |

A block hides everything in both directions: posts, comments, profiles and search results. It also ends the friendship and removes either person's tags on the other's posts.

Anyone can find you by your handle, whatever your default is, so friends can send you a request.

## Tagging who you trained with

On the finish summary, a "With" row sits next to Gym. You can only pick friends.

1. The post shows "with Jos" straight away, and Jos gets a notification.
2. When Jos accepts, the post also shows on Jos's profile, as a workout you did together.
3. Jos can remove the tag at any time. Unfriending or blocking removes it too.

Later, a tagged friend could log the same workout on their own phone from the post, starting a session with its exercises.

## Tables

These tables live in Postgres only. IDs are text. Times are Postgres timestamps, the same as Better Auth's tables, because these rows never go to SQLite. Every table points at `user` with `on delete cascade`, so deleting an account removes all of it.

| Column | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `user_id` | text, primary key | Points at `user.id`. |
| `handle` | text, unique | 3 to 20 characters of `a-z`, `0-9`, `_` and `.`. Stored lowercase. |
| `bio` | text, nullable | Up to 160 characters. |
| `photo_key` | text, nullable | Where the profile photo is in storage. Replaces the photo that only lives on the phone today. |

The display name stays on `user.name`, which Better Auth already owns.

## The feed

Your feed is your own posts, posts you can see from your friends and posts you're tagged on, newest workout first. It leaves out deleted posts and anyone you've blocked or who has blocked you. It pages with a cursor on `started_at` and `id`, so new posts arriving while you scroll don't repeat or skip rows.

Public posts from people who aren't your friends show on their profile, not in your feed.

## API modules

Following [API structure](/docs/api-structure), these are new modules:

| Module | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `profiles` | Your profile, someone's profile and their posts, and search by handle. |
| `friends` | Requests, accepting, declining, unfriending, your friends list and blocking. |
| `posts` | Sharing and resharing, the feed, deleting, media upload links, reactions, comments and tags. |
| `reports` | Reporting a post, comment or profile. |
| `notifications` | Your notifications, marking them read, and registering a phone for push. |

The `account` module's delete also removes stored media for posts and the profile photo, since the database cascade cannot reach storage.

## What it needs from sync

Very little:

- `post.workout_id` and `post_media.id` reuse the IDs the phone already makes.
- Deleting an account has to clear social rows and stored media as well as synced rows.
- Both need somewhere to store files. The tech stack keeps the API off Cloudflare's own storage products, so the provider still needs picking. Sync will want the same one if it backs up workout media.

## Building it

1. Profiles and friends: handles, search, requests and the friends list, on the Profile tab.
2. Sharing and the feed: sharing from the finish summary and history, media upload, a feed tab.
3. Likes and comments, with report and block in the same change.
4. Tagging who you trained with.
5. Notifications, in the app first and then push.

Comparing yourself with a friend is tracked in [#91](https://github.com/AGroupForCoders/Fatigue/issues/91).

## Still open

- **Where files are stored.** See above.
- **Moderation.** Who reads reports, and how fast. For a small app that is probably us, with an email for every new report.
