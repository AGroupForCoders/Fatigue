---
title: Scores and stats
description: How a set is scored when settings change how hard it is, and how the stats page shows progress across them.
---

On most trackers an exercise is one thing, so one line on a graph is enough. In Fatigue the same exercise comes in several versions: a brand, and settings such as a notch or a dead stop. 55 kg × 8 at notch 8 with a dead stop is a harder set than 55 kg × 8 at notch 7. This page is how we score that and show it. The decisions behind it are in ADR 0001 and ADR 0002.

## Settings

Settings sit under an exercise's name as one line, such as "Seat 4 · Notch 7 · Dead stop". Tapping the line opens them up to edit. A setting is a number, yes or no, or text, and it's saved per exercise and brand, since every machine is set up differently.

A group of sets can change a setting from its heading. A hold at notch 8 sits under "Hold · Notch 8" while the rest of the sets use the exercise's notch 7.

## The score

A set's score is weight × reps, made heavier by the settings that make it harder.

| Setting | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| A number, such as notch 1 to 10 | 1.0 at the worst end of its range, 2.0 at the best. Notch 8 is ×1.78. |
| Yes or no, such as dead stop | The factor you pick when it's on, ×1 to ×2. Off is ×1. |
| Text, such as "wide grip" | Never counts. |

Factors multiply and the total is capped at ×3. A set of 57.5 kg × 8 at notch 8 with a ×1.5 dead stop is 460 × 1.78 × 1.5 = 1,227.

A setting counts only once you say it does. Until then the score is plain weight × reps.

## Setting the weighting

You set it in three places:

- **When you add a setting.** One question: does it make the set harder? No, a little (×1.25), a lot (×1.5), or your own number. Numbers ask which way is better and the range instead.
- **In Edit exercise,** by tapping the setting.
- **From the graph.** Tapping a point shows its sum, and each factor in it can be adjusted from there.

Changing a factor re-scores all of history. Later, the app could suggest a factor from your own RPE: if dead stop sets at the same weight are usually a point of RPE harder, that's evidence of how much harder they are.

## The exercise page

Brands are never mixed on one line, because two brands' machines aren't comparable. A switcher at the top picks the brand.

Four metrics. The first two are what Hevy calls set volume and session volume, weighted by settings:

| Metric | What it is | Mixes settings? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best set | Your best set that day: its score | Yes, that's what the weighting is for |
| Session | Every set that day added up, each weighted | Yes |
| Weight | Your heaviest set | No, so it splits by setting automatically |
| Est. 1RM | Weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30) for rep sets | No, same as weight |

By default there's one line. **Split by** draws a line per value of one setting at a time: per notch you've used, or dead stop on and off. Filters pin another setting, such as notch lines with dead stop on only. Only one setting splits at a time, so the graph never becomes six tangled lines. The app remembers the split you chose for each exercise.

Splitting is also how you check a factor. If the dead stop line runs well above the plain one at the same weights, your factor is too high.

Under the graph are the lines with their best set and change, then records, then history.

## Records

Records come in two kinds, because the weighting is an interpretation and raw numbers aren't.

- **Across all setups, weighted:** best set and best session. These are the ones that say "you've never worked harder on this machine". They move if you change a factor, since they're scored with it.
- **Per exact setup, raw:** for each combination you've done, such as notch 8 with a dead stop, the heaviest set, the best estimated 1RM, and for holds the longest hold. These never move when a factor changes.

A record only competes within one brand. With "own progress per value" on a setting, each of its values keeps its own records, so notch 8 is never beaten by notch 7.

When you finish a workout, the summary lists what you beat: "Best set ever" for a weighted record, or "Heaviest at notch 8, dead stop" for a raw one.

## The Stats tab

A third tab in the bottom nav, between Workout and Profile:

- This week against last: workouts, sets and volume.
- Weekly volume for the last 12 weeks.
- Exercises you've done, each with a small trend and its change. Tapping one opens its exercise page.
